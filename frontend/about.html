Who we are

FX Bot is an automated trading platform that runs rules-based scalping strategies across crypto, gold and FX markets. You fund your account, the bot executes the trades, and you follow every position, your balance and your performance from a single dashboard in real time.

What we do

Our engine scans the markets continuously, generates short-horizon signals, applies strict risk controls before any order is placed, and streams live results to your account. You can learn more about the strategy on our How the Bot Works page, and review the risks on our Risk Disclosure page.
