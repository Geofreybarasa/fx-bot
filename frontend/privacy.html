Privacy & Security

FX Bot · Last updated 12 September 2026

Your privacy matters to us. This page summarizes the safeguards FX Bot uses to help protect customer data and account information.

1. Protecting your information

  • Encrypted connections — HTTPS helps protect information transmitted between your browser and our website.
  • Password protection — account passwords are stored as hashes rather than plain text.
  • Access controls — authentication and authorization checks restrict access to account information.

2. Keeping your account secure

Choose a strong, unique password and keep your sign-in details and verification codes private. Sign out on shared devices. If you suspect unauthorized access, contact support promptly.

3. Security limitations

No online service or storage system is completely secure. These safeguards help protect your information, but they cannot guarantee absolute security.

4. Privacy and security support

For questions about protecting your information or to report an account-security concern, contact us at support@fx-bot.example. Never include your password or verification codes in a support message.
